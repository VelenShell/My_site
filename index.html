About Me

Building AI is easy. Making it trustworthy is the work.

I'm one of three engineers on the AI team of a client project with 84 engineers and a 20-year-old codebase. Our platform serves about 100 users through a LiteLLM gateway, with Claude on Amazon Bedrock and Claude subscriptions.

My part: agents that analyse legacy data and propose business rules, a validation step that cross-checks every rule against Jira, Confluence and the code, PHI/PII guardrails for HIPAA, and helping people adopt AI without fearing they are being watched.

I came here from QA — backend, data and security testing since 2020. I design the logic, let coding agents implement it, and review the code and results myself. Knowing where the model is wrong is the actual engineering.
